**Deviations:** /code-review runs as a forked background skill. A foreground sleep was blocked, so I waited for it with a background command. I ran it on the PR number ("high 145"), not on `origin/main...HEAD`, because from a subagent that form reviews the orchestrator's checkout.
**Decisions not covered:** whether a cubic-time regex that only blows up on a hostile body under pull_request_target counts as a blocker. I counted it as a blocker: it is a CI regression from this round, it costs up to 6 h of runner time per event, and the fix is cheap. I also had to decide how to treat tightenings that only show up on probes and not in the 57-PR backfill. I classed them as "to fix: declare it".
**What the review found:** a blocker that both the round and my first review missed: the wrapper fix wrote two adjacent `(?:ws|wrapper)*` around `\n?`, giving O(N^3) backtracking (3 KB body = 4 s, 8000 spaces = 93 s). /code-review found it, not my manual probes. Also: `\b` before `sin` rejects `_sin tests: es config_`, which `main` accepted (it has been there since the first round and the first review missed it), and `**ADR unchanged:**` followed by the reason on the next line is now rejected and not declared.
**Proposal:** add to review-pr: "when a PR changes a regex that runs on input the author controls (PR bodies, titles), time it on long runs of the characters its quantifiers accept". Also: the backfill against merged PRs does not catch tightenings, so probe edge-case shapes against both the old and the new regex.
